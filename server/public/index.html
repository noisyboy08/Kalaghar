Frequently Asked Questions

How are products certified authentic? +
Every craft item on Kalaghar is Geographical Indication (GI) tagged and carries a QR code certificate tracing back to the master artisan's registered guild.
What is your delivery timeframe? +
Domestic India orders arrive in 3-5 business days. Express Air shipping is available in metro cities for 24-48 hour delivery. International worldwide shipping takes 5-8 business days.
What is your return & exchange policy? +
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy for undamaged items in original packaging. Simply request a pickup from your order tracking screen.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (VISA, Mastercard, AMEX), NetBanking, Razorpay, PayPal, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
